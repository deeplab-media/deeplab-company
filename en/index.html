Video production in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi

Deeplab Media (Deeplab Co., Ltd.) is a video production house with offices in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi, Vietnam. We produce TVCs and brand films, 3D/CGI product videos, corporate, hospital and event films, podcasts and monthly TikTok content, from idea and script to shoot and post-production. We reply in English, Japanese and Vietnamese, with quotes within 24 business hours.

FAQ

Can we work with you in English?

Yes. Deeplab replies in English, Japanese and Vietnamese, by email or Zalo.

Do you work with foreign companies?

Yes. Deeplab has produced films for Japanese clients such as Nichino, Ouji Hospital in Yamaguchi, the aluminium sash factory 日鋼サッシュ製作所 and DG Nozomi, and 3D product films for Dior, NIVEA and Apple Vision Pro.

Where do you film?

Crews are based in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi. Deeplab also films on location by project, including Hội An (Yaly Fashion Show) and several cities in Japan.

How much does video production cost in Vietnam?

It depends on scope: number of videos, shoot days, crew, talent, locations and post-production. Send your brief and you receive a detailed quote within 24 business hours, with no costs outside the quote.

Can you make a 3D product video without the physical product?

Yes. The product is rebuilt in 3D from drawings or photos, lit and rendered in Blender, then finished in After Effects. Changes are cheapest at the grey clay stage, before materials are added.

Do you offer white-label post-production for agencies?

Yes: editing, colour grading, sound, motion graphics, VFX and 3D. Deliverables carry the agency's name, Deeplab signs an NDA on request and does not contact the end client.

How long does a project take?

A TVC or brand film usually takes 2 to 6 weeks depending on scale; post-production for agencies takes 2 to 3 weeks.

Do you do game character animation?

No. Deeplab's 3D work is product video, 3D TVC and campaign key visuals, not character rigging or animation for games.
